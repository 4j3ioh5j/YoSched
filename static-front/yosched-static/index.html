Staff scheduling,

YoSched is staff scheduling software for teams that run around the clock. Automated, fair, and flexible — smarter schedules and happier teams.

Automated scheduling

Generate a balanced schedule in seconds, then fine-tune by hand. The engine respects your coverage rules, staffing limits, and each person's availability.

Fair by design

Built-in equity tracking spreads desirable and undesirable shifts evenly, so no one quietly carries more than their share month after month.

Time-off & shift requests

Staff submit their own time-off and shift requests. Approvals flow straight onto the schedule — no spreadsheets, no lost emails.

Coverage you can trust

Set minimum staffing and per-day limits. YoSched flags gaps and over-scheduling before they become a problem on the floor.

Hours on target

Track pay-period hour targets per person so everyone lands where they should — no surprise overtime, no short weeks.

Built for teams

Multiple schedulers can work at once with live coordination, so the person editing always sees the current picture.
